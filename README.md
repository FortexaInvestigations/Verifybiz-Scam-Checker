# Verifybiz — Scam Checker

Free Android app by Fortexa Investigations LLC. Enter a company name or a website address — Verifybiz checks it against official warning lists of financial regulators and other fraud databases. The check runs entirely on the device; the app collects no personal data.

**Get the app:** Google Play — https://fortexainvestigations.com/app/
**Same check in Telegram:** https://t.me/Verifybizbot

## Data sources

1. U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)
2. U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC)
3. Autorité des marchés financiers, France (AMF)
4. Bank of Russia — list of entities with detected signs of illegal activity
5. Swiss Financial Market Supervisory Authority (FINMA)
6. IOSCO — International Organization of Securities Commissions (I-SCAN)
7. Securities Commission Malaysia (SC) — Investor Alert List
8. Cybercrimepolice (Switzerland) — fraud warnings of the Swiss police
9. Destroylist / PhishDestroy — phishing domain database
10. Fortexa Investigations LLC — the agency's own investigations list (scammers from case breakdowns and blog articles)
11. ScamSniffer — crypto phishing domain blocklist (wallet drainers, fake airdrops, impersonated crypto projects)
12. Financial Commission — warning list of the forex dispute-resolution commission
13. Securities and Futures Commission of Hong Kong (SFC) — Alert List
14. Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) — fraudulent bank website warnings
15. Anti-Deception Coordination Centre (ADCC), Hong Kong Police — fraudulent investment platforms

Search works with transliteration: type a name in Cyrillic and the app finds its Latin-script entry, and vice versa.

## What Verifybiz is — and is not

Verifybiz is a preliminary check against public warning lists. A match means a regulator or database has flagged the name or domain — read the source entry before drawing conclusions. No match does not guarantee a company is safe: it only means the name is not on these lists. Verifybiz does not replace a professional investigation or due diligence.
